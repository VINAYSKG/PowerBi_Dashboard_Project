# Energy Sector
Built Power BI dashboards analyzing 50K+ smart meter records across 5 regions. Delivered insights on daily/night consumption (avg. 46.4 kWh), regional demand share (North 25.6%), and efficiency gap (13.9K kWh ≈14%). Designed forecasting and performance tracking dashboards to optimize energy utilization and monitor KPIs, improving decision-making in energy distribution.
